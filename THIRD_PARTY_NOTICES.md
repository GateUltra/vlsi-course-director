## Character artwork

The Placement source snapshot contains the original and adapted **Einstein** vector illustration by **j4p4n**, from [Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/detail/302755/einstein), identified there under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Its source record also names remix sources “Science Clipart 4” by scojoh and “Cartoon Eyes” by Prawny.

Original artwork, provenance and a saved license-source page are retained under `vlsi-html-course-studio/assets/placement-lesson06-source/course/assets/`. The closed-mouth treatment, articulated body and runtime gestures are described in the case study. The character does not imply the historical scientist's participation or voice.

## Course examples

Both skills credit Yongfu Li and the [EDA Learning repository](https://github.com/yongfu-li/eda-learning). The original Floorplanning skill is retained unchanged as a historical version.

The new Placement worked example includes an instrumented adaptation of the course's small placement model and update rules, together with newly authored narration, diagrams, UI and production code. The upstream course README expressed an intention to align with CC BY 4.0 and to add a LICENSE when publishing; the recorded checks did not retrieve a corresponding license document. That intention is **not presented as a verified license grant**, and this repository does not place the whole course-derived snapshot under an additional blanket license.

See the detailed [case credits](vlsi-html-course-studio/assets/placement-lesson06-source/course/CREDITS.md) and [snapshot scope](vlsi-html-course-studio/assets/placement-lesson06-source/SNAPSHOT_README.md). Preserve these records and verify the applicable rights when redistributing course-derived material. The skill and original starter can be used without adopting the Placement case study's model or assets.

No finished course videos, speech recordings or third-party music recordings are included. The audio-generation example identifies service voices and synthesizes its own music bed; use of an online service remains subject to its terms and the current user's authorization.
